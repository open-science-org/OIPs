## Abstract

Legacy OIP, originally written as GitHub issue #7. Higher validation layers would let research communities set their own validation rules while OSO maintains only the base layer. The issue asks how to prevent censorship and the manipulation of custom rules to suit a group's bias.

## Specification

None. The original text and discussion are in [issue #7](https://github.com/open-science-org/OIPs/issues/7).

## Rationale

The discussion argued that customization always introduces a bias toward some goal, which is often legitimate (by research interest, novelty, paper length or country). A group could create rules favoring its own ideas, but others are unlikely to use such a list. A group could also take over an established list and change its rules; researchers can then fork the list and keep their own rules, as with forks of a blockchain.

**Later developments.** The v1 design makes community setups public and versioned, and lets a community fork a setup and keep its ideas.

## Security Considerations

Capture of an established channel or sub-network by a group that changes its rules.
