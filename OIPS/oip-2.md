## Abstract

Legacy OIP, originally written as GitHub issue #3. It proposes funding OSO through grants, naming SBIR (NIH and NSF), the NIH Big Data to Knowledge program, R21 exploratory grants and Breakout Labs as targets.

## Specification

None. The original text is in [issue #3](https://github.com/open-science-org/OIPs/issues/3).

## Rationale

The issue argued that a grant would provide funding and signal to the scientific community that OSO had passed external scrutiny. It also suggested using the NIH's existing identification of grant applicants as an early identity source. It warned that relying on NIH infrastructure could push OSO back into the system it aims to change.

**Later developments.** The v1 roadmap places the first outside funding at milestone M3, after a legal entity exists.
